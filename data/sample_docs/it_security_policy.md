# IT Security Policy

## Password Requirements
All corporate accounts must use passwords that are at least 14 characters
long, contain a mix of uppercase, lowercase, numbers, and symbols, and are
rotated every 90 days. Passwords must not be reused across the last 10
rotations.

## Multi-Factor Authentication
MFA is mandatory for all systems that store customer data, financial
records, or source code. Approved MFA methods include authenticator apps
and hardware security keys; SMS-based MFA is discouraged for privileged
accounts.

## Data Classification
Data is classified into four tiers: Public, Internal, Confidential, and
Restricted. Restricted data (e.g. customer PII, financial records) must be
encrypted at rest and in transit, and access must be logged and reviewed
quarterly.

## Incident Response
Any suspected security incident must be reported to the Security
Operations Center within 1 hour of discovery. The incident response team
follows a 4-stage process: containment, eradication, recovery, and
post-incident review.

## Device Policy
Only company-managed devices with up-to-date endpoint protection may
access internal systems. Personal devices require enrollment in the
Mobile Device Management (MDM) system before any corporate access is
granted.
