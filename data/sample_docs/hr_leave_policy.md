# HR Leave Policy

## Annual Leave
Full-time employees accrue 24 days of paid annual leave per calendar year,
credited monthly at a rate of 2 days per month. Unused leave can be carried
forward up to a maximum of 10 days into the following year; any balance
beyond that is forfeited unless local law requires otherwise.

## Sick Leave
Employees are entitled to 12 days of paid sick leave per year. A medical
certificate is required for absences longer than 2 consecutive days. Sick
leave does not carry forward and resets at the start of each calendar year.

## Parental Leave
Primary caregivers are eligible for 26 weeks of paid parental leave.
Secondary caregivers are eligible for 4 weeks of paid parental leave.
Parental leave requests must be submitted at least 30 days in advance
through the HR portal.

## Work From Home Policy
Employees may work remotely up to 2 days per week with manager approval.
Fully remote arrangements require VP-level sign-off and are reviewed
quarterly.

## Approval Process
All leave requests must be submitted through the HR portal at least 5
business days in advance, except in emergencies. Manager approval is
required for leave exceeding 5 consecutive days.
